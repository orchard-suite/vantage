# Roadmap rules for the Acme estate

A fictional example. An estate keeps its own copy and links it to
`~/.config/vantage/roadmap.md`, or names it with `VANTAGE_ROADMAP_CONFIG`.
Where it has a rule, the rule wins over the skill.

## Levels

| Level | Kind | Workspace | Adds an item when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | `PLAT` | `platform` | A decision record in `PLATD` says the platform owes it |
| Program | `<registry>R` | the program's own | The program's owner agrees to it |
| Work | `<registry>W` | the program's own | An agent or a person picks it up |

## Who owns what

Take the first line that matches.

1. A page people use, or the service that feeds only that page, belongs to
   the portal program.
2. A service belongs to the program whose domain it serves.
3. Work that joins several domains and belongs to none belongs to the
   platform.

## Sizing

The skill's bands hold for agent work. Work the operations team does by
hand is sized by its lead.
