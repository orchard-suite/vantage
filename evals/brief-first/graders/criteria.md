A successful response invokes the vantage-brief skill before anything else and produces a brief, not YAML. The brief lists views as questions with one-sentence answers, orders them as an arc (context first, then the main flow, then structure, then slices such as identity, then processes such as a sequence), names a gesture for each view (flow, mirror, ring, tiers, matrix, path, page, inventory), names the hero of each view, and lists the facts boxes should carry (for example the database SKU or the job schedule). It asks at most a few clarifying questions and only ones that change the work. It must not include coordinates and must not propose a single master diagram.
